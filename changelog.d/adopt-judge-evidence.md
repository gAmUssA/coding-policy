### Changed

- **The judge checks cited evidence instead of investigating the tree.** Adjudication told the pinned judge, which runs on the most expensive model at max effort, to verify "the disputed facts against the tree itself" — open-ended investigation that diagnosis had already moved to the cheaper investigator seat. Each adjudication position now cites its evidence (a file and line, a command and its output, or a revision); the lead copies those citations into the new `POSITION_A_EVIDENCE` / `POSITION_B_EVIDENCE` placeholders of `templates/brief-judge.md` and never supplies its own. The judge checks only what is cited and never explores beyond it. A fact no citation settles gets the new ruling `insufficient — <facts needed>`: the lead runs an investigator consultation to establish exactly those facts with citations, then re-dispatches the judge on the same dispute with that report as `INVESTIGATION_REPORT`. `blocked` narrows to questions only the operator can answer. `insufficient` is not a completed ruling, so a checkpoint cannot cite it; `teamlead`'s checkpoint refusal now says so. Diagnosis mode is unchanged: it still rules on the investigator's assessment. Updated `references/round-flow.md` (The Judge, Ruling Outcomes), `references/team-operation.md` Judge Seat, `references/round-setup.md`, and `SKILL.md` Steps 11 and 17. Proven by `tests/test_compose_briefs.sh` 6f (the packaged judge brief composes with both positions' evidence and refuses without one; the accept case fails against the old template) and `tests/test_recovery.py` `test_an_uncompleted_ruling_cannot_be_cited_by_a_checkpoint` (fails against the old message). Idea from upstream jbaruch/coding-policy `8285c07` (0.3.264), ported to our tree.
- **The Herdr lead routes every edit and code review to a worker.** `SKILL.md` Step 1 let the lead "do it directly" for a single edit or existing-code review with `HERDR_ENV` set, contradicting `references/team-operation.md` "The lead reads the shared checkout and never edits it". With `HERDR_ENV` set the lead now answers a question or one bounded read-only lookup itself, citing what it read, and sends any edit or code review on to Step 2; standalone mode is untouched. Moving edits and reviews off the lead also keeps crew work out of the longest-lived, top-model context. `tests/test_skill_invocations.sh` check 5 rejects a direct-finish Step 1 branch naming an edit or review, and requires an edit branch that routes to Step 2; its fixtures prove the old Step 1 text is rejected and the new one accepted. Adapted from upstream `d546c76` (nonworking foreman) and `67678a0` (bounded factual lookup), keeping our cheaper direct answer for questions and lookups.
